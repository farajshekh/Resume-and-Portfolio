01 — About

Where research meets the road.

Transportation engineering demands mathematical discipline, geometric precision and rigorous asset management. My work sits at the junction of all three.

I pair doctoral research at IIT Gandhinagar with hands-on consultancy as Deputy Engineer at LEA Associates South Asia — from geometric alignment and corridor capacity studies in Autodesk Civil 3D, to pavement structural design under IRC:37, to spatial mapping in QGIS.

  • III

    Longevity
